# Portfolio Track

**Portfolio track:** Engineering Management Research  
**Public presentation:** Research in System Engineering  
**Repository:** `empirical_engineering_tradespace`  
**Focus:** Systems Engineering / Trade Space Analysis / Decision Analysis

This repository is one of four Engineering Management Research repositories in the eight-repository empirical research portfolio.

On the public portfolio site, it is presented under **Research in System Engineering** because the research question, decision framing, and method are systems engineering oriented.

The portfolio classification and the public research heading serve different purposes:

- **Engineering Management Research** identifies the portfolio track.
- **Research in System Engineering** identifies the research area shown to readers.

The repository should not be reclassified as AI Engineering or Learning and Development research.
