## How these references are used

The systems engineering references motivate explicit trade space construction and decision maker preferences.

The concrete references establish that mixture design naturally involves competing performance and resource objectives.

They are not used to claim that the present repository is the first use of Pareto analysis in concrete design. The contribution claimed here is narrower: a transparent observed alternative analysis focused on sensitivity to objective definition.
